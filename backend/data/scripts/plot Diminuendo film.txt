thumb|400px|right|Haskell and The Doll Set in the year 2025, the film stars Richard Hatch as Haskell Edwards, a film director who has struggled with drug and alcohol abuse since the suicide of his girlfriend, Cello Shea (Dykstra) nine years earlier. Edwards is offered the chance to direct the story of Cello's life in a biopic being produced by LifeForm, a company which makes unintelligent, but utterly lifelike, "LifeDolls" of various celebrities. The film will star their LifeDoll of Cello, and as Haskell sets to the work of re-living his past to make the film, he becomes ever more obsessed with the machine, believing it to contain the real Cello's spirit.  The film touches on themes of loss, love, obsession and redemption.
