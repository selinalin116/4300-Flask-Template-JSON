Seafield (Ryland Brickson Cole), an eccentric, alcoholic, and self-entitled captain, assembles a team of specialists in what he refers to as the "Team of the Century". The team consists of weapons expert Sean Shaughnessy (Erick West), sonar operator Nedge Pepsi (Beulah Peters), and so-called former Navy Officer Dick Flynn (Daniel Long). With Seafield in charge, they set out to track down and kill the title monster to avenge the death of his father. After several failed attempts, Seafield is forced to take matters into his own drunken hands.
