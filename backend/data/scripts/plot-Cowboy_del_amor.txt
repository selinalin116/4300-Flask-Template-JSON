Ivan Thompson is a 60-year-old former horse breeder who found his calling and career as The Cowboy Cupid.  His business is match making.  Specifically, introducing American men who are interested in finding love and marriage to Mexican women intent on the same.  For US$3000, Thompson will escort you to Mexico and upon arrival arrange introductions with eligible ladies.  The success rate is high.  But before the client is accepted and before a trip to Mexico can be made, the client must first pass Thompson's "smell test".  The film chronicles his attempts to set up three men. The first is a 48-year-old truck driver who lives on a mobile home next to his parents’ house, whom he successfully introduces to a 20-year-old seamstress from Torreón. They marry and have a child.  Another client is an American used car dealer who does not hit it off with a Mexican doctor.  The last is a toothless veteran who marries a Mexican laundress.  Finally Ivan quits the business and decides to go town to town in Mexico to pursue his own quest to find love.
