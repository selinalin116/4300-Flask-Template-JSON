Charlotte and Rob Sanders, living a modest life in a small apartment, feel overshadowed by the seemingly perfect life of Charlotte’s college friend, Jackie Jennings. Jackie’s annual Christmas letter, detailing her family's extraordinary achievements, incites envy and skepticism in Charlotte.  Charlotte, feeling her own family is unremarkable, describes her daughter Dora's obsession with Marvel comics, her son Grant's attachment to a stuffed monkey, Rob's impractical dream of house flipping, and her own abandoned inventor dreams. When Grant, misunderstanding his mother's sarcasm, navigates the family to the Jennings' home for Christmas, they find themselves snowed in and forced to stay.  Charlotte suspects Jackie's family, especially after meeting Jackie’s fit, Latino husband, Valentino. Her distrust leads her to snoop around their house. Meanwhile, the children, influenced by Beatrix's skepticism, embark on a misguided mission to expose a mall Santa, culminating in a confrontational scene.  Charlotte accuses Jackie and Rob of having a secret affair, based on misinterpreted correspondence. This leads to a revelation of Rob's harmless tradition of sharing Jackie's Christmas letters with their family, kept secret due to Charlotte's known disdain for them.  The film's climax involves an elaborate solar-powered hot air balloon, a tribute to Jackie's late son, Daniel. A series of events results in a Christmas pageant spectacle, where Santa's sleigh is accidentally attached to the balloon.  In the film’s epilogue, the Sanders and Jennings families collaborate on a joint Christmas letter. Charlotte boasts about her new invention, while the Jennings promote their non-governmental organization (NGO) through solar-powered hot air balloon travel.
