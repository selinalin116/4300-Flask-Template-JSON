Cale Bryant is a young man who delves deeper into his passion, debuting in the world of Motocross.  Now that he is an adult, he decides to choose his own future, for once, independently, to help him become a champion with the support of parents and his girlfriend.  After hard training, Cale decides to enroll in the National Championship for amateur motocross, convinced that he can win. In the last race, Cale is in the lead until the last lap where his bike breaks down. Cale beats his arch rival Derek Black by pushing his bike across the finish line but is disqualified for pushing his bike across the line. Derek celebrates his victory until it is revealed that Derek had been disqualified for causing a wreck and Cale will receive the pro contract for next season.
