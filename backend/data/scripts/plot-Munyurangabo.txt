After stealing a machete from a market in Kigali, Munyurangabo and his friend Sangwa leave the city to return to their villages. Munyurangabo seeks justice for his parents, who were killed in the 1994 genocide against Tutsi in Rwanda, while Sangwa wants to return to the home he had left years ago. Although the two boys had planned to stay for only a few hours, they end up spending several days. But, because they are from two different tribes, their friendship is sorely tried. Sangwa's parents distrust Munyurangabo, and warn their son that Hutus and Tutsis are supposed to be enemies.
