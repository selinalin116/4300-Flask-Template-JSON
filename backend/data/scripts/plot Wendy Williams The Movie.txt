Wendy Williams is a teenage girl growing up in New Jersey.  Cathy Hughes hires Williams to work in Washington, D.C., at the WOL radio station. Williams takes cocaine to suppress her appetite. She also gets a weekend job in New York City as an emcee. By 1990, she's working full-time in New York City. Hot 103.5 becomes Hot 97.  Williams remains addicted to cocaine during her marriage to Kevin Hunter. However, she finally quit cocaine.
