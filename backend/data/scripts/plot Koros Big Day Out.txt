Koro's Big Day Out[a] is a 2002 Japanese animated short film written and directed by Hayao Miyazaki.[1] The film is about Koro the puppy, who runs away from his mistress, experiences some adventures around town and who is finally happily returned home. The film is exclusively shown at the Ghibli Museum and Ghibli Park, both in Japan.
