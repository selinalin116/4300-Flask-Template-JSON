Composer Peter Bretter is in a five-year relationship with actress Sarah Marshall, who stars in television show Bretter composes music for. Sarah abruptly announces that she is breaking up with him. Devastated, Peter, against the advice of his stepbrother Brian, takes a trip to Hawaii. However, the vacation is ruined when he learns that Sarah and her new boyfriend Aldous Snow are also guests of the resort. Taking pity on him, hotel concierge Rachel Jansen offers him an expensive suite for free.  Peter begins spending time with Rachel and starts to develop feelings for her. Meanwhile, the relationship between Sarah and Aldous begins to falter.  Aldous and Peter run into each other and Aldous inadvertently informs Peter that he and Sarah began having sex a full year before she broke up with Peter. When Peter confronts Sarah, she confirms it, while Peter begins to realize his relationship with her wasn't as great as he remembered. He begins a sexual relationship with Rachel to get back at Sarah and Aldous' overt displays of affection. This progresses to a point where Aldous is overwhelmed by Sarah's competitive streak and they break up. Later, when Peter is consoling her, Sarah attempts to seduce him into rekindling their relationship. He leaves her after showing her that his body has started to reject her amorous overtures.  He flies back to Los Angeles and,begins working on new project. He sends an invitation to Rachel for opening night. Rachel eventually accepts. After the successful performance, she congratulates him and tells him she's looking into attending college in the area.
