The film follows four storylines, which each focus on one particular couple. They are titled:   "The Mama's Boy" vs. "The Single Mom"  "The Non-Committer" vs. "The Girl Who Wants the Ring"  "The Dreamer" vs. "The Woman Who Is Her Own Man"  "The Player" vs. "The 90 Day Rule Girl"  Each of the women are readers of Steve Harvey's book Act Like a Lady, Think Like a Man. When the men learn that the women are reliant on Harvey's advice, they try to turn the tables on their mates, which later seems to backfire.
