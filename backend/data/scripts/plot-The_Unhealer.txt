Kelly has a rare eating disorder called Pica, which causes him to eat inedible items. He gains supernatural powers when a faith healer named Pflueger tries to heal him but dies during the healing ritual. Kelly's powers allow him to transfer pain inflicted on him to his aggressors, which he uses to take revenge on his bullies. However, he later learns to take responsibility for his actions and tries to banish his powers, only to make things worse.
