Captain Kuppa: Desert Pirate (砂漠の海賊！ キャプテンクッパ, Sabaku no Kaizoku! Kyaputen Kuppa, "The Desert Pirate! Captain Kuppa") is a 26-episode anime series that aired from 2001 to 2002.

Produced by Bee Train,[1] it was licensed in English by Enoki Films.[2] The anime aired on NHK BS2 on August 13, 2001.[1] In 2002 Enoki Films announced on its website that it had licensed the series.[3]
