"Clean Shaven"  Archana is a woman who is forced into a conservative married life. When her husband realizes that she has shaved her pubis, he decides to imprison her as a punishment.   "Afterbirth"  A mother struggles to recognize her newborn son as a human creature.    "Dance Dance Dance"  Rio, a young break dancer from the Bronx is rejected by his family after telling them that he is gay. He is forced to leave his parents' house and live on the streets.   "Love of Love"  Members of a close-knit Japanese clan end up performing an orgy in a sordid a sex club.   "Love of my life"  Flashbacks evoke the story of a long relationship.    "I do"  A young bride has a panic attack shortly before celebrating her wedding. What helps her is an unexpected encounter with a man from her past.
