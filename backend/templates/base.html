    What is DNM?

    DrinkMovieNibble is a project that sought to solve an everyday issue. You want to watch a movie but have to fit in time for dinner... why not do both at the same time? We've created the perfect app for you to use to find those perfect recipes and those perfect cocktails to enjoy while watching your favorite films. Watch some Mission Impossible with a martini in-hand. Have a hamburger while watching Pulp Fiction!

    This app uses various basic NLP methods to return relevant results based on ad-hoc queries. This may or may not include Jaccard/Cosine Similarity, SVD, BERT, Rocchio's, Word2Vec, etc.
